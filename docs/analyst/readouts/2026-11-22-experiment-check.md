## Next iteration

Across the two most recent complete UTC weeks (November 8–14 and 15–21), ordinary weekly active people went from 246 to 204 and median memes per feed session stayed at 14. Each week had 21 non-automated signups. Attributed non-self share clicks fell from 10 to 5, and share-acquired signups from 5 to 2. These are product-level trends across the mix of experiments. [Canonical definitions](../north_star.sql)

1. Use the weekly push for randomized training examples. Join each accepted outcome to the exact chosen item, its candidate set, its selection probability and its decision-time features. Keep all first assignments in the online comparison. At each weekly data check, report how many *additional mature, non-top* examples the new policy produced.
2. Run the next monthly offline evaluation on a later, fully mature time window. Keep the current model frozen on the test period, compare against the current HQ baseline on supported candidates, and report effective sample size and uncertainty before training a challenger. Keep feed fallbacks with unknown probabilities outside propensity-weighted policy evaluation. A promising offline result qualifies a model for a randomized online test.
3. Prioritize one follow-up feed change for people who *have already reacted* to a push. Test just the first subsequent feed selection, with a prespecified return-to-feed outcome and a fixed follow-up. For organic acquisition, measure first tap and reaching five memes in a separate onboarding cohort. The current weekly push experiment retains its original question and sample.
